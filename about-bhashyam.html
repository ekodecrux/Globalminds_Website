Bhashamites, Now World Over.

Global Minds has completed over two and a half decades in the realm of education, and along the way the institution has produced numerous achievers and inspirers who continue to make their mark across the globe today.

What started as an initiative to impart education to enable success, has today taken shape and has spread into a pedagogic institution that is providing children a head-start to become our alumni who shall inspire value and vision in the future. As we are proud of our past generation of students, we look forward to this generation of achievers as well, who we believe will be the next set of Global Minds students dotting the world achievers' map.

A Modest Dream Turns into a Mission.

Today, lakhs of successful Global Minds students stand as torch-bearers of its unmatched legacy of education the world over. This unparalleled journey of over two-and-a-half decades has been enthralling with vigour, endeavour and goals to create achievers in every generation.

Here is a teacher who has a vision for the future. He sets for himself a goal to spread education to as many students as possible and groom generations of young minds to achieve their life goals. Global Minds School was born about 29 years ago from the dreams of Shri Bhashyam Ramakrishna, the chairman of the group and a visionary with his focus on the future. Today, the group has spread its footprints to several places across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

  • Holds unparalleled record for highest number of centums in Math and Sciences with best grades in the state till date.

Strengthening the Roots of our Branches.

To keep pace with increasing academic aspirations and changes in the world, there are four Global Minds schools, with each focusing on a certain age-group of students. It starts with the Kindergarten and goes right up to preparing students for India's top Medical and Engineering colleges. Each institution is like a preparatory ground for the next level of education, making it easier for the child in the next academic step.

Global Minds started its journey in 1993 with 186 students, with pioneering educational concepts to students from Grade VI to X. As it grew in name and stature, it established exclusive Primary wing schools. It later started innovative foundation courses for aspiring students in Engineering and Medical streams. Today, the group has emerged as the largest educational group with over 1,41,000 students across the various portals of Global Minds.

Global Minds Public School, since its inception is known for its success-oriented programme for Grade VI to X. The curriculum imparted in these schools has been upgraded to suit the new competitive education pattern prevailing in the country. The focused teaching-learning approach followed here makes it a numero uno institution in South India.

To set a strong learning foundation from the formative years itself, the group has set up Global Minds Little Champs with classes from KG to Grade V. The exclusively designed programs offered here set a perfect launchpad for children to aim for higher levels of knowledge and learning after primary education.

Global Minds IIT Foundation Academy delivers a focused and result-oriented 5-year integrated foundation program from Grade VI onwards to nurture students towards success in JEE (Main, Advanced), BITSAT, IIST, KVPY and NEST.

To suit the new Medical competitive education format in the country, Global Minds initiated MEDIAIMS, a dedicated Medical Excellence Programme that is aimed to groom students for NEET from their VIII Grade.
